# Product Overview

Numa AI Therapist is a compassionate AI-powered therapy companion that provides emotional support and mental health assistance through intelligent conversations. The platform combines artificial intelligence with evidence-based therapeutic approaches to deliver accessible, personalized mental health support.

## Core Features

- **AI-Powered Conversations**: Empathetic responses using advanced language models
- **Emotional State Analysis**: Real-time emotion detection and mood tracking
- **Session Management**: Comprehensive therapy session recording and analysis
- **Goal Setting & Tracking**: Personalized mental health goals with progress monitoring
- **Context-Aware Responses**: AI maintains conversation context and user history
- **Privacy-First Design**: End-to-end encryption and secure data handling

## Target Users

- Individuals seeking accessible mental health support
- People looking for supplementary therapy tools
- Users who prefer AI-assisted emotional guidance
- Those interested in CBT-based therapeutic techniques

## Key Principles

- **Compassionate**: Warm, empathetic, and understanding interactions
- **Evidence-Based**: Grounded in CBT, DBT, and mindfulness practices
- **Privacy-Focused**: Secure handling of sensitive mental health data
- **Accessible**: Available 24/7 without traditional therapy barriers
- **Personalized**: Tailored responses based on user history and context
